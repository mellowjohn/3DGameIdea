9. **Faction armor families:** Tessera = base medieval; Cristallo = Númenórean / Italian elegance, feather plumes, blue accents, etched plate, sub-faction style variation; Imperium = Warhammer-adjacent silhouette **with blocky retention**.
10. **Imperium ladder:** skirmisher → footsoldier → heavy/elite mobs affirmed.
11. **Luceran hollow:** crown-off → human/dead-self idea affirmed; Nazgûl / “medieval Voldemort” hollow kit ok; GoodPlayerModel blocky Luceran preferred over high-fid sheet for production.
12. **Damius:** peasant walker rejected; needs **leather + cloak + some heraldry** (scout operation-dependent).
13. **Ashfell Blade starter:** must read **warrior / fighter** (mail or plate resemblance even early) — current kit reads “pleb” vs Outrider/Runecaster; Dom: max Ashfell should feel swan-knight / aura.
14. **Underflow orcs:** clan identity required (Warcraft / Warlords clan differentiation). Underflow → water / deep-sea emblem; optional sacrificial / tentacle-arm corruption variants; banners; skin/eye corruption variation.
15. **Nefarium mineral:** Dom — **pure black** (contrast). Red crystal massing in Act 3 concept is cool but may be a **different resource** if not Nefarium.
16. **Claritas:** tallest part of Cristallo island concept affirmed as direction (exact pin still D-P2-08).
17. **Sandbox product feel:** linear campaign + lots of side content / world interaction; Dom wants a strategy call with **George**.
