# Ashfell Blade (Starting Archetype)

- Status: draft player-character context (owner lock 2026-07-24)
- Decisions: [DEC-0009](../decisions/index.md#dec-0009-starting-archetype-character-creation), [DEC-0044](../decisions/index.md#dec-0044-starting-archetype-lane-orgs-and-rename)
- Former name: **Squire** (superseded display name)

## Role

**Ashfell Blade** is the melee starting archetype. Players begin as the steel arm of **House Ashfell** — a draft noble-house obligation under King Asher’s war levy — not as a generic “fighter class.”

See [Character Creation](character-creation.md) for the shared protagonist premise and the other starting archetypes.

## Home org

- **House Ashfell** (draft display name; Dom may confirm or rename).
- Theme: Asher’s war-levy clients — banner line and conscript scrap under one roof.
- **Sub-themes:** **Fighter** (formal contracts / levy steel) and **Brawler** (street muscle / irregular war).

## Background

An Ashfell Blade begins as an ordinary person from a settlement under the Kingdom of Tessera. King Asher the Brittle has ordered every able-bodied person into the war against the Chaotic Imperium. The character is drafted before escaping and sent to the front lines after the Imperium breaches the supposedly impenetrable fortress of Calrenoth.

Asher replaced the remembered “Great King” Luceran, whose fall and transformation into the Usurper are central to the conflict.

**Twine Act 0 / Dom lock 2026-07-22:** Creotar’s vision calls Asher Luceran’s **fragile half-brother** on the throne. **Kinship is established** (D-P0-13). Brittle/absent support remains **draft** character color.

## Goal and Social Obligation

The Blade is expected to satisfy House Ashfell — gaining power or influence for the house — while the house later **binds** into broader Cristallo / Kingdom / tribal politics ([DEC-0044](../decisions/index.md#dec-0044-starting-archetype-lane-orgs-and-rename)).

The archetype begins from fear and obligation rather than conventional heroic confidence.

## Progression

- Ashfell Blade → Fighter-lean or Brawler-lean mastery inside the house, then advanced archetypes by morality / allegiance milestones.
- Draft long-term forks (Knight / Warrior paths) remain placeholders until specialization design lands.

Detailed advanced archetypes are deferred until after the demo under the current plan.

## Leadership Struggle

An Ashfell Blade player may grow into a future leader or king on either a good or evil path. Relationships and faction standing determine which options remain viable. Charisma strongly influences how others judge these decisions.

## Relationships

Companion selection directly affects the protagonist's relationships and development. The story is intended for an M-rated audience and may include mature relationships and emotionally intimate or quasi-romantic bonds depending on character development.
