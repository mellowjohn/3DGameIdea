# Runecaster (Starting Archetype)

- Status: draft player-character context (owner lock 2026-07-24)
- Decisions: [DEC-0009](../decisions/index.md#dec-0009-starting-archetype-character-creation), [DEC-0044](../decisions/index.md#dec-0044-starting-archetype-lane-orgs-and-rename)
- Former name: **Acolyte** (superseded display name)

## Role

**Runecaster** is the magic starting archetype. Players begin in the **Runecaster Guild**. They are casters whose power comes from **creating runes** and **drafting sigils** (item/rite/boon-native), not from a seminary “mage school.”

See [Character Creation](character-creation.md) for the shared protagonist premise and the other starting archetypes.

## Home org

- **Runecaster Guild**
- Theme: rare inscribed magic — prepare, etch, and trigger power under guild rules.
- **Sub-themes:** **Rune** (prepared etched slots) / **Sigil** (live-drafted mid-fight)

## Magic boundary

Supernatural power in Tessera is deliberately limited ([story-vision.md](story-vision.md)). Runecasters fit that boundary: power is authored into runes/sigils and foci, not blanket racial casting.

**Not this archetype:** the Cristallo crystal-guardian order (White Lotus draft label — rename open). Those guardians are a mostly **non-magic** custodian / political path and must stay separate from the Runecaster Guild.

## Background

Shares the drafted-to-Calrenoth premise with Ashfell Blade and Outrider. Guild mentors and inscribed kit differ; the war does not.

## Progression

- Runecaster → Rune / Sigil lean inside the Guild, then advanced archetypes by morality / allegiance milestones.
- Draft long-term forks remain placeholders until specialization design lands.
- Later **bind** into major faction politics per [DEC-0044](../decisions/index.md#dec-0044-starting-archetype-lane-orgs-and-rename).
