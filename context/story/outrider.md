# Outrider (Starting Archetype)

- Status: draft player-character context (owner lock 2026-07-24)
- Decisions: [DEC-0009](../decisions/index.md#dec-0009-starting-archetype-character-creation), [DEC-0044](../decisions/index.md#dec-0044-starting-archetype-lane-orgs-and-rename)
- Former name: **Archer** (superseded display name)

## Role

**Outrider** is the ranged starting archetype. Players begin in the **Outrider Lodge** — a scout / skirmish company under the war levy — not as a generic “archer class.”

See [Character Creation](character-creation.md) for the shared protagonist premise and the other starting archetypes.

## Home org

- **Outrider Lodge**
- Theme: wartime scouts and skirmishers ahead of the line.
- **Sub-themes:** **Ranger** / **Forager** / **Nomad**

## Background

Shares the drafted-to-Calrenoth premise with Ashfell Blade and Runecaster. Kit and Lodge mentors differ; the war does not.

## Progression

- Outrider → Ranger / Forager / Nomad lean inside the Lodge, then advanced archetypes by morality / allegiance milestones.
- Draft long-term forks (Marksman / wilderness paths) remain placeholders until specialization design lands.
- Later **bind** into major faction politics per [DEC-0044](../decisions/index.md#dec-0044-starting-archetype-lane-orgs-and-rename).
